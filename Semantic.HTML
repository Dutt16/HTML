School Info

Iselin Middle School, located at 900 Panther Way in Iselin, NJ, is a public school in the Woodbridge Township School District serving grades 6-8 with a population of approximately 800-866 students. It offers a 12:1 student-teacher ratio, with 54% math and 51% reading proficiency rates. The school provides a Gifted & Talented program and extracurriculars like choir.
